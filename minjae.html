MINJAE

The Hacker / Vocalist / Backup Rapper

Minjae is a bio-enhanced human with neural relay implants that once made him useful to NuYu. Before E.V.E.N, he worked inside the company as a low-tier systems admin, trusted with parts of the code no one thought to protect, obedient and invisible, another disposable name badge. Learning how NuYu’s systems fit together, he quickly understood where they didn’t.

Before he disappeared, Minjae sabotaged one of NuYu’s internal systems. By the time the company realised what he’d taken with him, he was already gone. His hands are scarred from the breach, where he burned his way out to freedom.

What surfaces in his music now isn’t just rebellion, but understanding.

Mainly serving as the band’s backup rapper and co-songwriter, Minjae breaks apart belief systems as effortlessly as he once cut through firewalls. Fans say that you don’t listen to Minjae, you decode him.

There’s a rumour floating around online that he once rapped so fast in binary it crashed a venue’s mainframe. Minjae has never confirmed it. He just smirks like it could be true.
