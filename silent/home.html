Silent Cartographers write maps for the people who stayed. Their music sits under the skin of The Ghost Lines: grainy, bruised, stubbornly tender, carrying the kind of beauty that only grows where the world stopped polishing the edges.

SoundPost-rock crescendos, alternative guitar weight, room noise, tape hiss, found percussion, soft vocals buried low in the mix.
Visual languageTorn gig posters, photocopied maps, rust stains, damp paper, scratched type, warm light caught in ruined places.
OriginNo one seems entirely sure where Silent Cartographers came from. Their recordings appear and disappear across old relay frequencies, often attached to route archives, forgotten signal logs, or damaged map files. Most tracks are untitled in their original uploads. The names came later.
