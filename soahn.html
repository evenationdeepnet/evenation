SOAHN

Vocalist / The Empathic Core

Soahn is one of E.V.E.N's vocalists and often the first person to notice when something has changed. He pays attention to details that other people might dismiss and carries that awareness into everything he does, from the music he writes to the quiet routines that have become part of life in the Ghost Lines. Thoughtful and introspective, he has a way of helping people feel understood without ever making a spectacle of it.

Before arriving in London, Soahn worked for NuYu's research division in Germany, where he became permanently linked to an emotional AI known as SEER-9. The connection remains with him today, existing almost like a second consciousness beside his own. It allows him to experience emotions with unusual intensity and shapes the way he moves through the world, sometimes beautifully and sometimes overwhelmingly.

Life in the Ghost Lines has given him a place where that sensitivity feels understood rather than burdensome. He gravitates towards quiet rooms, abandoned platforms and moments of calm between busy days, often becoming the person others instinctively seek out when they need to slow down for a while.

Although he can appear reserved at first, there is a quiet playfulness to him that emerges around the people he trusts. He enjoys small rituals, shared meals and the routines that have grown naturally between the members over time. Those moments matter to him because they turn an abandoned city into a home.
